# `plan` workflow

After a spec exists, a design and plan turn it into actionable work. Review them before implementing.

Relevant crtr skills (load with `crtr skill show <name>`):

- `review-design` — verify the design covers requirements, simulate flows, check feasibility
- `review-plan` — verify the plan covers requirements and matches the design

Use after creating a design or plan, before implementation.
